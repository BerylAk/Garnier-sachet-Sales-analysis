# Garnier-sachet-Sales-analysis
## Project Overview

This data analysis project examines sales performance of Garnier haircare sachets across three key African markets—Nigeria, Ghana, and Kenya—over a 24-month period (2023–2024). By analyzing transaction-level data across distribution channels, regional performance, and pricing dynamics, the project identifies market anomalies and uncovers strategic insights into why Kenya dominates volume despite lower formal retail presence, while Nigeria's high distribution density fails to translate to revenue leadership.

## Data Sources

Sales Data: The primary dataset comprises 16,998 transaction records spanning January 2023 through December 2024, covering Garnier sachet SKUs across formal (modern retail, supermarkets) and informal (street vendors, kiosks) channels in Nigeria, Ghana, and Kenya.

Supplementary Data: Regional demographic profiles, L'Oréal subsidiary performance metrics, and channel-level trade spend budgets inform contextual analysis and ROI calculations.

## Critical Findings

 1. Kenya's Informal Channel Dominance — Despite lower formal retail footprint, Kenya leads regional volume through dense informal distribution networks and higher per-transaction volumes in street and kiosk channels.

2. Nigeria's Distribution Paradox — High formal retail distribution points (supermarkets, modern trade) do not correlate with revenue leadership; informal channel underpenetration limits total addressable market capture.

3. Ghana's Margin Erosion — Competitive pricing pressure and channel fragmentation reduce per-unit profitability, positioning Ghana as a volume-challenged market requiring promotional intervention.
